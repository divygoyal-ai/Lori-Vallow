# Lori Vallow series

True-crime vertical (9:16) series built with HyperFrames. Episode 1 is finished and approved.

**Before doing anything, read `docs/HANDOFF.md` (workflow, rules, lessons) and `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md`
(binding production rules, caption spec).**

Non-negotiables, in one line each:
- No faces in any AI shot (people from behind only); no minors' faces anywhere; real footage 70-80%, AI 20-30%.
- Always ask the producer for the exact credit cost and get a yes before any paid Magnific operation
  (especially video upscaling). Use Kling 2.5, not 3.0, unless told otherwise.
- Use the local HyperFrames CLI (`npx -y hyperframes@0.8.79 ...`), not the HyperFrames MCP.
- Commit and push work to the session's branch; do not open a PR unless asked.
